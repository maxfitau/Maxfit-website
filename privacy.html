Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

MaxFit is a personal training business run by Max French in Inner West Sydney. This page explains what information MaxFit collects through its membership card, app and website (together, "the app"), why, and how it's kept. If anything here isn't clear, email maxfrenchfitness@gmail.com and ask.

What we collect

  • Contact and membership details — your name, phone number, email, and your training package and session balance.
  • Attendance and bookings — when you check in, what sessions you've booked, and your booking code.
  • Workout data — the exercises, weights, reps and sets you log, and the workouts Max assigns you.
  • Nutrition data (if you use Fuel) — food you log, including photos of meals and barcodes you scan, your macro targets, body weight, and goals you set.
  • Referral details — if someone refers you, or you refer someone else, we record who referred whom so loyalty rewards work.
  • Payment confirmation — if you pay for sessions or a Fuel plan online, Stripe (see below) tells us the payment succeeded and what it was for. We never see or store your card details.
  • Reviews — if you choose to leave a review, we collect your name, star rating and review text.

How we use it

Strictly to run your training: tracking your sessions and attendance, building and logging your workouts, giving you nutrition feedback, handling bookings and payments, and operating the loyalty and referral programs. We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Meal photos and AI

If you scan a meal in Fuel, the photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to estimate what's in it and its macros. The photo itself is not stored afterwards — only the food and macro estimate it produced. Barcode scans are checked against the public Open Food Facts database.

Who we share it with

  • Google — the app's data is stored in Google Sheets, run through Google Apps Script.
  • Stripe — processes payments for sessions and Fuel plans. MaxFit never handles or stores your card details directly.
  • Anthropic — processes meal photos and food descriptions to estimate nutrition, as above.
  • Open Food Facts — a public, open barcode database used to look up packaged foods.

We don't share your information with anyone else, and we don't sell it.

Keeping it secure

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure, including restricting who can access the parts of the system that hold private data. No method of storage or transmission is ever perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security — but we treat your data carefully and keep private information (like food logs) separate from anything shared more broadly.

Your choices

You can ask to see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, at any time — just email maxfrenchfitness@gmail.com. If you stop training with MaxFit and want your data removed, we'll do that on request, aside from records we're required to keep for tax or legal reasons (like payment records).

On your device

The app saves a few small things directly on your phone (like your member link and whether you've seen the welcome tour) so it works smoothly without a login. This stays on your device and isn't sent anywhere.

Children

MaxFit's services are for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact
