About

Meet your coach

Max, Founder & Coach

Tap to read the full bio

6 years training Cert III & IV Fitness · NHFA Certified 2026 Psych (Hons) · UTS

I've been training for 6 years, driven by a strong passion for fitness. What I love most is helping people fall in love with fitness the way I have, not just chasing results, but enjoying the process. I'm genuinely fascinated by what drives motivation, real connection, and the kind of conversations that shift someone's mindset. Those interests are drawing me toward a Bachelor of Psychology (Honours) at UTS, and they shape how I coach every day.

As a student, you find yourself in meaningless jobs to get quick cash, like a cafe or a grocery store. Instead, I'd prefer to build something real. My interest in psychology has found me exploring connection, motivation, conversations, and mindset, all essential aspects of building your fitness. So why not do something you love? For me, that's fitness, and helping others. If you want to come with me on my journey, I'd love to join yours to a happier, healthier, and stronger version of yourself.

At Max Fit, training is simple, structured, and tailored to you. With the latest science-based training, I do the hard part for you, enhancing your results without the guesswork. Magical results. Except it's not magic, it's science.

Whether you're training from home, in a gym, a park, or wherever life throws you, consistency always wins. I'm in your corner 100% of the time. Whether you're a beginner working out from home or you've had 10 years of experience stuck in old habits, we use science to find your maximal results.

"Because everybody deserves the peace of mind that comes from a healthy body, and the ability to apply the same skills to crush whatever life throws at you."
