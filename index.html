Meet your coach

At Max Fit, training is simple, structured, and tailored to you. With the latest science-based training, I do the hard part for you — enhancing your results without the guesswork. No gimmicks. No confusion. Just optimal programming and real results.

Whether you're training from home, in a gym, a park, or wherever life throws you, consistency always wins. I'm in your corner 100% of the time. Whether you're a beginner working out from home or you've had 10 years of experience stuck in old habits, we use science to find your maximal results.

03

Group Classes

Small group sessions that bring energy, accountability, and community to your training. Every class runs 45 minutes and follows the same MaxFit arc — a mindfulness opener, a dynamic warmup, a main training block, a dedicated cardio finisher, a core finisher, and a guided stretch cooldown.

Circuit

Heart & Hustle

The most cardio-forward class on the timetable — station circuit work paired with a Tabata sprint finisher to keep your heart rate up the whole session.

EMOM

Mission: Slimpossible

A weight-tone class built on EMOM blocks (every-minute-on-the-minute strength work) with a dedicated cardio ignition blast.

Tabata

Strong & Sculpted

A hypertrophy-focused session — Tabata-paced strength work to build and sculpt, finished off with a heart-rate cardio block.
