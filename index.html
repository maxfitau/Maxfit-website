About

Meet your coach

Most people trying to get fit have been here before: a generic program off an app, a burst of motivation, then life gets busy and it quietly falls apart. You're not lacking willpower, you just haven't had a plan built around you, with someone actually holding you to it.

At Max Fit, training is simple, structured, and tailored to you. Backed by 6 years training myself and Certificate III & IV in Fitness, I do the hard part for you, enhancing your results without the guesswork. Magical results. Except it's not magic, it's science. I'm driven by a strong passion for fitness. What I love most is helping people fall in love with fitness the way I have, not just chasing results, but enjoying the process. I'm genuinely fascinated by what drives motivation, real connection, and the kind of conversations that shift someone's mindset. Those interests are drawing me toward a Bachelor of Psychology (Honours) at UTS, and they shape how I coach every day.

Personally, I could be working a forgettable job just to get by. Instead I chose to build something real around what I love: fitness, and helping people grow. My interest in psychology, exploring motivation, connection, and mindset, is a big part of why. If you're ready to stop guessing and start seeing results, I'd love to help you get there.

FAQ

Questions, answered

01

How much does personal training cost in Inner West Sydney?

One-on-one sessions are $70 for 30 minutes, $90 for 45 and $110 for 60. Duo sessions (two people, one coach) are $50, $60 or $75 per person. Group classes are $30 a class and online coaching is $40 a week. Your first session is free, there are no lock-in contracts and no hidden fees. Full pricing is in the Pricing section above.

02

What qualifications does your personal trainer have?

I hold Certificate III & IV in Fitness through NHFA, plus a current First Aid certificate, and I'm a WeFlex Accredited Fitness Professional and Active Support Worker, trained to coach people with disability. I'm insured, and I've spent 6 years training in the gym myself. I'm also working toward a Bachelor of Psychology (Honours) at UTS, which feeds directly into how I coach.

03

Do you train people with disability?

Yes. I'm a WeFlex Accredited Fitness Professional and Active Support Worker, trained to support people with disability with a focus on inclusion and empowerment. Every plan is built around your goals, your body and your pace. Book your free session and we'll talk through what you need.

04

Do you offer online personal training?

Yes. Online coaching gets you a custom weekly program with regular check-ins, wherever you train. Current online clients range from right here in Sydney to as far as Thailand.

05

Can you train me at home or in the park?

Yes. One-on-one sessions run at a park or gym near you, and group classes rotate weekly through Hawthorne Parade, Sydney Park, Rushcutters Park, and Wentworth Park.

06

What areas do you serve?

In-person training covers Inner West Sydney, including Haberfield, Newtown, Glebe, and nearby suburbs. Online coaching is available anywhere.
